# FinTrust Digital Bank – Data Analytics Project

This repository documents my Data Analytics contribution to the
**FinTrust Digital Bank – Financial Intelligence & Digital Banking Support Solution**
completed as part of the **AnalystLab Africa Experience Lab**.

My work is organised into two submitted stages:

- **Week 1 – Understand & Plan**
- **Week 2 – Analyse & Prepare**

## Project Overview

FinTrust Digital Bank is a fictional digital banking organisation
serving customers through mobile and online banking channels.

As the organisation's customer base grows, it collects increasing
amounts of customer and transaction data. The business requires
stronger analytical capabilities to transform this information into
actionable business intelligence and support better operational
decision-making.

The wider FinTrust project brings together Data Analytics,
Data Science, Machine Learning Engineering, Generative AI and
Project Management.

## Business Problem

FinTrust has customer and transaction data but requires better
visibility into:

- Customer behaviour
- Transaction behaviour and patterns
- Products, channels and transaction types generating activity
- Potential financial-risk indicators
- Operational and management decision-making

## Project Objective

The overall project objective is to develop a data and AI solution
that helps FinTrust understand customer and transaction behaviour,
identify potential financial-risk patterns and support operational
decision-making.

Within the **Data Analytics Track**, my responsibility was focused on:

- Data exploration
- Data cleaning
- KPI development
- Customer analysis
- Transaction analysis
- Dashboard development
- Business insights

## Key Business Questions

The analysis explored questions such as:

- Who are FinTrust's major customer segments?
- How do transaction behaviours differ across customer groups?
- What transaction patterns are most important to management?
- Which transaction types generate the greatest activity?
- Which banking channels generate the greatest activity?
- How do transaction values vary across customer segments?
- How do transaction statuses vary?
- What patterns are associated with transactions requiring risk review?

## Data Quality Summary

The analysis used:

- **1,500 customer records**
- **12,000 transaction records**

`Customer_ID` was used as the customer-level identifier and as the
relationship key between the customer and transaction datasets.

During data preparation:

- Missing `Device_Type` values were identified and handled.
- Missing `Location` values were identified and handled.
